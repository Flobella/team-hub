Deal workflow

What to do the moment something moves.

Under contract

  1. Submit the contract intake formIn the team's Teams channel, right after execution. Don't wait.
  2. Ella opens the ReZen transactionYou don't open it yourself. Ella does it once your intake is in.
  3. Upload docs for compliance in ReZenAll deals are filed and approved for DA in ReZen.
  4. Update the lead in LoftyMove it to Under Contract so the pipeline stays accurate.

New listing

  1. Submit the listing intake formIn the Teams channel.
  2. Check out lockbox and signs with EllaOnly after the listing intake form is submitted.

Fees & dates

Team agent fees and Real Broker transaction fees.

Team fees

  • $45Monthly fee, due the 15th of every month. No grace period.
  • AnnualAnnual fee, due every November 1, no matter when you joined.
All team fees are non-refundable.

Transaction fees

  • $695Transaction fee, paid by the client. If the client doesn't pay it, the agent does.
  • $0No transaction fee on rentals.
  • $1,000Flat fee on your personal transactions.
The team doesn't offer bonuses of any kind, including agent, buyer and seller bonuses.

  4. Sponsor: whoever brought you inIf two people brought you in together, list both.
